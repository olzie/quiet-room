# Quiet Room

A tiny bedtime game. You're already in bed, and that's all you need to do. You can't lose here, and nothing is rushing you.

**Play:** https://olzie.github.io/quiet-room/

## What's in the room

- **Dumpling the cat** in a witch hat. Stroke his back slowly and he falls asleep right on the bed.
- **The window**: a breathing exercise with the moon. It grows as you breathe in and shrinks as you breathe out.
- **The curtain** can be drawn or opened. The moon still glows through it.
- **A branch in a vase** blossoms little by little as you water it.
- **A jar of paper stars**: write down what's spinning in your head, and the note folds into a star and stays in the jar.
- **Laundry by the basket**: a small mess that turns into a tidy stack.
- **The lamp**: turn it off and close your eyes.

There's also a soft background noise, and the cat purrs while you pet him.

## Languages

English, Ukrainian and Russian. The game picks your browser language, and you can switch with the button in the top corner. You can also open a specific one with `?lang=en`, `?lang=uk` or `?lang=ru`.

## Tech

One self-contained `index.html` with no build step: hand-drawn pencil-style SVG, plain JavaScript and the Web Audio API. Progress (the blossoming branch, the stars in the jar, the curtain) is saved in your browser only.
